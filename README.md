# Call-rotation- 106 rule 10am-6pm only Created by Joel W
If you have a team of three or more, distribute incoming calls fairly among team members.


Rotation rules are the following:
1) John
2) Mary
3) Joel
4) back to 1

QA Regulations:
A. Who will answer the first call? This depends on who took the last call on previous day.  For example, I took the last call for the day, then the next person will be John.

B. Let’s say if you are the only one who starts 9am-10am You own the schedule. You must pick up all the calls. Don’t let others pick the call unless you are on the phone. If they don’t pick up, that’s their fault. If there were no calls during that time, you would be the first candidate to answer call at 10am.  


C. Lunch time break: we follow the general order, in this example John->Mary->Joel, and then after lunch time we set a new order based on who took the most/least calls. So, if Joel took 5 calls today, then John 4, and Mary 2, then Mary would take the first call, then John, then Joel. This also applies for those who leave at 5pm or earlier.

Exceptions:  
(1) Let’s say John took the call during my lunch break and Mary didn’t take any call. When I come back from lunch, Mary will be next. 

(2) Let’s say I was supposed to take next call but there were no calls before I left for lunch. The next person will be John to take my call and continue the rotation between 2 people. 

(3) let’s say you need someone to take your call because you are away. You will ask the team for a favor to take your call. Whoever helps you, you will take his/her call next (pay back the favor) then continue rotation. Pretty much taking 2 calls.

(4) Day off or vacation: Whoever took PTO will start the call on their return day then rotation.
